# Content brief — `/de/` retire-in-panama

**Source article:** `/living/retire-in-panama` (`updated_on` 2026-08-09, 12 sources, 8 FAQs)
**Brief written 2026-08-16.** Every figure below was read from primary text today, not taken from the English row.

---

## 1. The German competitive set

Ten German SERPs. The same ~12 domains recur, and **not one of them is a publisher first.** The market is lawyers and residency consultants doing content marketing (anwalt.de ranks #1 or #2 on almost everything; vidalibreplan, fischer-cie, FRAPAN, agroupc), plus insurance brokers who own the health cluster outright (insurancy.de takes positions 1, 2 and 4 on the *Krankenversicherung* query), plus a thin layer of resident blogs (immogration, panamacommunity, auswandern-handbuch).

There is no German International Living. That is the gap.

## 2. Table stakes — omit any of these and the page reads as incomplete to a German reader

Present on effectively every ranking German page:

- $1,000/month lifetime pension, with **"lebenslang garantiert"** stated explicitly
- +$250/month per dependent
- Territorial taxation, phrased as *"die deutsche Rente bleibt in Panama steuerfrei"* — the single most repeated claim in the corpus
- **USD as currency, framed as *Wechselkursrisiko entfällt*.** A German-specific anxiety the English page never addresses
- The Pensionado discount schedule, itemised
- **Unbefristet** — permanent, not temporary, stressed as a contrast
- The process must run through a licensed Panamanian lawyer; you cannot self-file
- Boquete / Coronado / Bocas / El Valle as the location shortlist
- Spanish needed for *Behördengänge*
- **German GKV does not cover you**

Three of those (the discount schedule, the *Wechselkursrisiko* framing, the GKV point) are **absent from our English page** and have to be written fresh, not translated.

## 3. What no German page carries — our openings

- **Derecho de posesión.** Zero coverage across ten SERPs. Unoccupied ground, and the site's central claim.
- **CSS / Caja de Seguro Social.** The broker that owns the health SERP never mentions Panama has a public system.
- **Any primary Panamanian citation.** One statutory reference across ten SERPs.
- **The health-insurance age curve.** Retirement pages quote $100–300/month to an audience that is by definition 60+; the one honest German page says $600–1,000 for 65+.
- The 2-year presence rule.

## 4. Figures verified from primary text today

### Pensionado threshold — SNM requirement sheet, read 2026-08-16
> *"En caso de que el solicitante ha adquirido una propiedad a título personal en el territorio nacional **por una suma superior a los cien mil balboas (B/.100,000.00)**, la pensión podrá ser por un mínimo de setecientos cincuenta balboas (B/750.00)."*

🚩 **fischer-cie.de tells German readers this threshold is measured on the *Katasterwert*.** The statute says the sum the property was *acquired for*, personally. Cadastral value is routinely far below purchase price, so their version materially misstates who qualifies. **Correct this explicitly.**

Also confirmed in the same sheet: B/.1,000 monthly *concedida en forma vitalicia*; spouses may combine; **B/.250/month per dependent** (item 8 — so our English page is right); dependent children temporary to 25 while studying; and *"Este Permiso es de forma indefinida por lo que no requerirá de prórroga"* — which is the sourced backing for the *unbefristet* claim every German page makes without a citation.

Legal basis as printed: Ley 9 de 1987, Decreto Ley 3 de 2008, Decreto Ejecutivo 320 de 2008 arts. 200–203 (mod. DE 26 de 2009), Ley 38 de 2000.

⚠️ The **"$170,000 invested for 5 years"** alternative that appeared in one German snippet matches nothing in the primary text. **Do not repeat it.**

### The discount schedule — Ley 6 de 16 junio 1987, MICI copy, read 2026-08-16
Artículo 1 covers *"panameños **o extranjeros residentes** en el territorio nacional"*, women 55+, men 60+, and all jubilados/pensionados. Resident foreigners are explicitly included.

| Benefit | Statute |
|---|---|
| Recreation, cinema, theatre, sport | 50% |
| Air fares | 25% |
| Interurban buses / trains / boats | 30% each |
| Hotels | **50% Mon–Thu, 30% Fri–Sun** |
| Restaurants | 25%; fast-food franchises 15% |
| Private hospitals and clinics | **15% of the total bill — only if the holder is *not* covered by hospitalisation insurance** |
| Prescription medicines | **10%** |
| Medical consultations, general and specialist/surgical | 20% |
| Dentistry / optometry | 15% each |
| Technical and professional services | 20% |
| Prosthetics and mobility aids | 20% |
| Closing costs, personal and commercial loans | 50% |
| Mortgage interest, own-use housing | 1 point |
| Passports | 50% |
| Electricity | 25%, account in own name and bill ≤ B/.50 |
| Telephone fixed charge | 25%, residential, one line, own name |
| Water | 25%, own name, ≤ B/.10/month |

🚩 **German pages say 20% on medicines. The statute says 10%, and only on prescription.** Repeated across the market.

🚩 **The 15% hospital discount is conditional on *not* having hospitalisation insurance** — a condition no German page carries. This is the strongest single insight available for this page, because the same German pages that advertise the discount also sell the private policy that voids it. Say so plainly and let the reader do the arithmetic.

⚠️ The utility discounts have caps (B/.50 electricity, B/.10 water) that make them close to irrelevant for a foreign retiree's actual bills. State the caps rather than listing the headline percentage.

**Register: `Sie`.** The split in the market is by business model — the blogs use *du*, everything selling to money uses *Sie*. Our reader is 55+ with pension income, and *Sie* also travels to Austria and Switzerland, which are a third of DACH clicks.

**Freshness is table stakes.** Roughly half the ranking pages carry a year in the `<title>` and a visible *"Zuletzt aktualisiert"* line.

## 6. The angle

German readers are being sold a $1,000 threshold and a discount list by people who profit from the application. Meanwhile the one readable German complaint thread is a buyer who **lost a deposit on a house purchase with a lawyer present** (auswandererforum.de/threads/25883). The market's content and the market's actual pain are not addressing each other.

So: give them the sourced version of what everyone else asserts, correct the two figures the market has wrong, tell them the hospital discount dies the moment they buy the insurance those same pages sell them, and bridge to *derecho de posesión* — which is the thing that actually costs Germans money and which nobody writes about in German.
