# Page triage: keep, rewrite, or redirect

Goal: hold the traffic that exists, then spend effort on new content and on
improving what earns. Every one of the 56 published articles gets one of three
dispositions.

Traffic figures are GSC impressions over the full 16-month window, attributed
to the v2 URL through the live redirect map, so a page that absorbed four v1
URLs carries all four. Read them with `14-v1-traffic-coverage-audit` in hand:
most of this volume is synthetic long-tail, and on real named queries the site
sits at position 40 to 95.

## The three tiers

| tier | rule | pages | impressions | clicks |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **T1 rewrite** | ≥100 impressions, or ≥1 click, or a P0/P1 role in the pillar map | 32 | 16,205 | 47 |
| **T2 keep, revisit later** | 20 to 99 impressions, no clicks, no map role | 10 | 383 | 0 |
| **T3 redirect** | <20 impressions, no clicks, no map role | 14 | 134 | 0 |

T1 holds 97% of impressions and every click. That is where rewriting pays.

## The blocker on T3

Most of the redirect list **cannot be redirected yet**, and the reason is worth
being explicit about rather than discovering halfway through.

The natural destinations are the consolidation pillars in
`06-pillar-cluster-map.csv`: `/living/living-in-panama`,
`/living/moving-to-panama`, `/residency/panama-residency-guide`,
`/buying/buying-property-in-panama`. **None of them exist yet.** They are all
"Create" rows.

Redirecting a thin page into a loosely-related existing page is not a
workaround. `netlify.toml` already records why, from the `/es/` decision: bulk
redirects to loosely-related pages get treated as soft 404s, so the equity is
lost anyway and a working page is replaced with a worse signal.

So T3 splits:

**Redirect now — genuine same-topic matches, 3 pages:**

| from | to | why |
|---|---|---|
| `/living/expat-depression-panama-unfiltered` | `/living/why-expats-leave-panama-2-years` | Same subject. The map already says expand the destination into the drawbacks pillar. |
| `/living/supermarkets-shopping-panama-expats` | `/living/panama-food-guide-expats` | Groceries and food, same reader, same question. |
| `/living/what-to-pack-moving-to-panama` | `/money/real-cost-of-moving-to-panama` | Both are move-logistics. |

**Blocked until a pillar exists, 7 pages:** `panama-for-families-with-children`,
`moving-to-panama-from-florida`, `things-to-do-in-panama`,
`getting-around-panama-city-guide`, `panama-visa-rejected-what-to-do`,
`pedasi-rising`, `santa-catalina-panama`. Leave live and untouched. They cost
nothing where they are.

**Reclassified out of T3, 4 pages.** The rule caught them but judgment says
keep:

- `/buying/avenida-balboa-panama-real-estate` (0 impr) and
  `/buying/colon-panama-real-estate` (2 impr) are June 2026 pages, written for
  v2. Zero traffic because they are new, not because they failed.
- `/buying/condos-for-sale-panama-buyers-guide` (15) and
  `/residency/panama-golden-visa-2026` (12) both sit on real commercial terms in
  `04-keyword-universe.csv`. Low impressions here means not ranking yet, which
  is an argument for rewriting them, not for deleting them.

That leaves the honest sequence: **build the four pillars, then redirect the 7
blocked pages into them.** Not the other way round.

## Images

Articles have no images. `og_image_path` exists per-article in
`0001_init.sql:300` and the article template already renders `next/image`, so
the schema and the component are in place. What is missing is the pipeline:
generate, upload to R2 via `scripts/upload-media-r2.mjs`, store the path.

The R2 half already works and is proven by the project photos. The generation
half does not exist for this site. Extending `panama-writer` with an image step
modelled on the ChefGoya recipe skill is the right shape, with two adaptations
that matter here:

- **Never generate a photorealistic image of a named real property.** A
  synthesised picture of a real building presented as that building is the image
  equivalent of the testimonials just removed. Project and area photography must
  stay real, from Airtable and R2.
- Generated imagery belongs on the abstract guides, where nothing is being
  depicted as a specific real place: closing costs, visa processes, banking,
  cost-of-living comparisons. Diagrams, maps and charts over stock-style scenes.

Not started. Flagged as a skill change, not a content task.
