### 1.2 The August impression surge is mostly not people

Impressions went from ~150/day to 1,000–1,500/day on 2 August. The queries driving it are shaped like this:

- `"panama" "pet birds" import permit quarantine` (30 imp, pos 5, 0 clicks)
- `asep panama internet providers fiber plans 2024` (6 imp, pos 10)
- `panama city panama furnished 2 bedroom apartment rent 2024 expat guide` (pos 11)
- `cable onda tigo panama fiber plans 2024 mbps` (6 imp, pos 7)
- `panama health system minsa css official` (8 imp, pos 6)

Nobody types those. They are the retrieval queries LLM research agents issue when a user asks ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini "how do I bring my parrot to Panama". Our pets page has 4,289 impressions and 1 click; the internet page 2,843 and 2. Two things follow:

1. **Do not read the impression graph as progress.** Human demand is flat.
2. **Being the page those agents retrieve is a real asset**, because they cite it in the answer. It is worth checking whether the guides carry `dateModified`, author, and clean tables (they do), but it is not a lead channel yet.

### 1.4 What is working, in miniature

Pages that already earn page-1 human clicks (90 d): `/de/articles/retire-in-panama.html` (7 clicks, **410 status**), `/de/articles/10-best-places-to-live-in-panama-2026.html` (5, pos 4.7, 410), `/projects/silverbay-panama` (pos 7), `/projects/margaritaville-playa-caracol` (pos 6), `/buying/boquete-panama-real-estate` (pos 8.5), `/buying/condos-for-sale-panama-buyers-guide` (pos 7), `/areas/boquete` (pos 14), `/money/atm-cash-panama-guide` (pos 6).

Read that list again: **project pages and area pages rank on page 1 almost immediately** on brand-plus-place terms with zero competition ("silver bay panama", "margaritaville playa caracol", "bioma costa del este"). Guides do not. That is the single most useful fact in this document and §4 is built on it.

Germany is our #3 country by clicks (17) and every one of those clicks landed on a 410. Panama itself is #2 (27 clicks), which is Spanish-language and local demand we serve only in English.

### 1.5 The funnel is not the bottleneck

`components/article-cta.tsx` puts a category-matched broker ask at three points in every guide, accepts phone/WhatsApp, and attributes `page_path`. Two leads from ~150 real sessions. Leave it alone; the work is upstream.

Portals asked about that are dead: panamarealestate.com (DNS), panamaproperties.com (expired cert), prpanama.com (expired domain). compreoalquile 403s all bots.

**The publishers (IL, Live and Invest Overseas, Panama Relocation Tours, Expat Exchange):** all funnel to newsletter → paid report/conference/tour, none to a broker. IL's Boquete real-estate page is stamped *November 2018* with 2018 prices. LIOS keeps its neighbourhood cost tables behind the $120/yr Panama Letter paywall. Panama Relocation Tours is **sold out through 2026** (one seat left in Oct, one in Jan 2027) — that is unserved demand with nowhere to go. Expat Exchange is templated across countries and 403s crawlers.

**The brokers (Casa Solution ~500 posts, Panama Equity ~370 + ES, Punta Pacifica Realty ~1,100 news posts, PanamaRE 13k listings):** volume, listings, prices, WhatsApp on most. Casa Solution has 60+ community pages and the deepest area text; Panama Equity has the freshest market-data posts (Q1 2026 report, El Cangrejo guide 22 Jul 2026). Weaknesses across the set: undated pages, no drawbacks, area pages that are listing grids with a paragraph, and blogs that read as news clippings. Only Kraemer (law firm) qualifies leads by budget; only PPR offers consultation booking.

**The structural gap nobody fills:** no ranking domain combines (a) dated, bylined, sourced English guides with (b) priced, honest project/area pages with drawbacks. Portals have inventory and no editorial; law/tax firms have editorial and no inventory; brokers have both but templated and undated. We already have both surfaces built. What we lack is authority and about 40 pages.

**SERP-by-SERP beatability** (from the teardown; full table in the agent output, summarised):

- *Beatable now (weak top-3):* can foreigners buy property in panama (Wise / Facebook post at #3), moving to panama from usa (two shipping companies in top 3), best places to live in panama for expats (#2 is a 2023 IL page), panama pacifico real estate (a homepage at #2), bocas del toro real estate (a hotel blog at #3), panama property tax exemption (blog tag pages), panama closing costs (templated 2026 pages, contradictory figures), buying property in panama (#1 stamped 2025).
- *Medium:* coronado, pedasi, costa del este apartments, boquete real estate, pensionado visa requirements (law firms; one still says $750/$100k), friendly nations visa (broker already ranks #4).
- *Hard, skip as primary targets:* best places to retire in panama (Forbes/USNews top 3; #4–8 beatable), cost of living (Numbeo/Wise), panama real estate for sale (pure listing intent).

Neither Reddit nor any .gob.pa source appeared in the 29 SERPs. panamarealestateguide.com appeared in none.

### 3.3 Fill the commercial-intent gaps (new pages)

1. **Terms with a broker at the end of them** (pensionado, FNV, closing costs, "can foreigners buy") beat lifestyle terms with more volume.
2. **Terms where the SERP is visibly weak** (see §2) beat higher-volume terms held by Forbes.
3. **Place pages beat guides** on this domain (see §1.4). Every town/neighbourhood with a broker inventory gets an area page before it gets an article.

### 3.4 Convert the impressions we already have

Cheap, do alongside Wave 1:

- **Title/dek CTR pass on the eight pages sitting at position 6–12** with >200 impressions and <1% CTR: internet-providers, moving-with-pets, healthcare-costs, atm-cash, safety, weather, sending-money, buying-property-in-panama. Front-load the number or the year (`Panama Internet 2026: Tigo vs +Móvil vs Starlink, prices`). Rules in `panama-seo-metadata-rules`.
- **Internal links from the traffic pages to the money pages.** The pets/internet/ATM guides carry the impressions; the pensionado/closing-costs/area pages will carry the leads. Every living/money guide gets a "Where expats actually buy" module linking three area pages.
- **Project pages: 13 have `has_price_source: false`.** Price is what makes them rank and convert; get the sheets.
